# Art source

The shipped Unity scene is a code-built 2.5D sunlit plush boutique. Its coral felt rug, mint quilt
runway, embroidered canopy, open thread shelves and round plush creatures are procedural low-poly
geometry. The generated PNG assets in `public/g/mungsil-fashion-show/assets/` are retained as early
concept provenance only and are not used by the WebGL scene.

Palette and form source: `factory/work-lanes/20261009-013916-L3/chosen.json` and `art.json`.
Round-2 redesign source: `Scripts/MungsilView.cs`. It restores the chosen plush/felt identity,
rebuilds the title start object as a world rope, separates wide information and control zones, and
adds a 1-to-6 practice drag demonstration. Pointer evidence and its artifact hash live under
`validation/`.
