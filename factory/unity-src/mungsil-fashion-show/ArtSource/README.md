# Art source

The shipped Unity scene is a code-built 2.5D midnight pattern atelier. Its dark cutting table,
angular garment forms, pinned pattern wall and wardrobe rails are procedural low-poly geometry;
the generated PNG assets in `public/g/mungsil-fashion-show/assets/` are retained as early concept
provenance only and are not used by the WebGL scene.

Palette and form source: `factory/work-lanes/20261009-013916-L3/chosen.json` and `art.json`.
Round-1 redesign source: `Scripts/MungsilView.cs`; pointer evidence and its artifact hash are in
`validation/pointer-playthrough-results.json`.
