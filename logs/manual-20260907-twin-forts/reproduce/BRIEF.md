## Core
Grade 6 semester 2 unit g6s2-u4 비례식과 비례배분 (read actual standards). Split an integer troop total N across LEFT and RIGHT lanes in stated ratio a:b. Main input drag a divider in the deployment strip; shows current left and right troop numbers and visibly redistributes troops in arena. Precision +/-1 buttons (transfer one troop, 44px) and keyboard arrows complement dragging. No multiple-choice answers and no pre-submit correctness highlight/auto-solve. Deploy button commits allocation, battle follows. Exact predicate left*b === right*a with left+right===N and nonnegative ints. Generator guarantees integer solutions. Show ratios clearly with consistent left/right lane colors. No fake question-only pool: samples derive from actual generator/pool. General sample diversity >=70% for QA 40/17/63.

## Round loop
9 assaults across 3 distinct arena phases (landscape/scenery and battle difficulty visual variants). First fixed guided 30 troops ratio2:3 (left12/right18), excluded from general first-attempt metrics. Later random unique equations from substantial real pool (>=200), total around12..90, both sides at least3, ratios1..9 incl non-reduced/equal and switching larger left/right. Planning has no clock pressure. Commit valid distribution -> brief 2.5–4s animated troop march/tower fire/impact/castle defeat -> readable success/partial-unit explanation -> next assault. Wrong -> visible asymmetric battle loss + one home-heart lost, same question retained, explanation of total/(a+b) then multiplier only AFTER wrong if desired; revise and retry. 3 wrong submissions -> real failure + restart. Ordinary one-error path can still win. Combat should be legible cause/result, not random defeat despite correct math. Show first-try bonus and per-round progress, actual local best and won banners/fort decoration persisted safely, no storage access crash. Final victory and failure differentiated, one-click replay.
